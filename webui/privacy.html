Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This explains what Rova collects when you use the website and app, why, who it's shared with, how long it's kept, and how to delete it. Rova doesn't sell your personal information.

What we collect

  • Your account: email, username, display name, and your password (stored only as a one-way hash), plus when you accepted these terms.
  • Your taste profile: the answers you give in the optional survey (who you go out with, budget, food rules, things you love or avoid, whether you drink).
  • What you ask the planner: the messages you type, the settings you choose, the area you plan around, and the plan or answer you got back.
  • Location: only if you turn on live location. It's used to find places near you, and the coordinates of each plan are kept with that plan (see how long below). You can always type a neighbourhood instead.
  • Usage: which days you used Rova and when you were last active, plus technical logs (errors, response times and, at our hosting provider, IP addresses) used to keep the Service running and secure.
  • Plans you share: when you tap “Share the night”, that plan (its title, stops, times, addresses and costs) is stored with a link. Anyone with the link can see the plan and its picture card; they can't see your name, email or account.
  • Event listings: if you submit an event, your name (optional) and email, so we can contact you about it.
  • Waitlist: your email and name, if you join it.
  • Website analytics: Vercel Web Analytics counts visits and pages viewed without cookies and without storing anything that identifies you. Only if you accept the cookie banner, Google Analytics also records visits and pages viewed.

What's stored on your device

Your sign-in token, the plans you save, and your planner settings are kept in your browser's local storage so the app works and remembers them. Google Analytics cookies are set only if you accept them.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and build plans that fit what you asked for and your taste profile.
  • To keep the Service working, secure and free of abuse.
  • To understand how Rova is used and improve it (for example, how often plans find nothing).
  • To send emails you ask for (password resets) or expect (a waitlist invite).

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect people's safety or Rova's rights. If you buy tickets through a link, the seller's own privacy policy applies.

How long we keep it

  • Planner requests (including their coordinates): 90 days, then only daily totals without personal details are kept.
  • Server statistics: 90 days.
  • Your account and taste profile: until you delete your account.
  • Plans you shared: until you delete your account, which also takes their links down.
  • Event submissions: while the listing is relevant, or until you ask us to remove it.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn live location off at any time, or never turn it on.
  • Change or clear your taste profile in "My taste".
  • Delete your account in the app; your account, profile and history are removed.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it, by emailing us. Depending on where you live (for example under Canada's PIPEDA or the EU/UK GDPR), you may have further rights, including complaining to a privacy regulator.

Security

Passwords are hashed, connections use HTTPS, and access to the database and admin tools is restricted. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting you, we'll tell you as the law requires.

Children

Rova isn't meant for children under 13, and nightlife features are for people of legal drinking age.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll show the new version in the app. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: a contact email is coming soon. Until then, you can change your taste profile or delete your account (and its history) yourself in the app.
